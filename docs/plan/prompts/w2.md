## What the real club changed

The structure arrived on 2026-08-25 and the model was checked against it. Most of it fits
untouched, and two things do not. **Both land in `enrollment`, which this wave's contract
commit is where it is built — so they must be settled here, not discovered in W4.**

### Fits with no change

* **Different hours on different days.** Tuesday 15:00–17:00 and Friday 15:00–19:00 is two
  `group_schedule_rule` rows, each with its own `start_time` and `end_time`.
* **1-on-1 sessions are §5.8 events, not sessions.** Events are already first-class,
  targetable at an individual student, fee-bearing, attendance-taking and visible in both
  apps and the calendar feed. M2 does not model private lessons at all.
* **Upgrading a student between groups** is one `enrollment` ending (`ended_on`, status
  `ended`) and another starting. The history survives in the rows.
* **A student in two groups sees both groups' sessions.** The parent schedule is the union
  of their enrollments; artboard `1a` already groups by day and can show one child twice.

### C11 — pricing is per student, not per group

§5.10 attaches a `price_plan` to a **group** and the billing run creates one charge **per
active enrollment**. The club prices per **student**, by how often they train — roughly 300
for twice a week, 500 for daily — independent of which groups those sessions belong to.

Under the spec's rule, a student in the competition group *and* the teenagers group gets
**two tuition charges every month, at two different group prices**, silently and forever.

§5.4's opening line makes it worse by asserting *"each child is enrolled in one group"*. The
schema never enforced that, §3.3 describes `enrollment` as a link table, and the club
contradicts it outright. **That sentence is wrong; the link table is right.**

Two consequences for the contract:

1. The billing run must produce **one** tuition charge per student per period, not one per
   enrollment.
2. The amount must hang off something the manager sets **per student**.

### C12 — a student may attend only some of their group's weekly sessions

§5.7's four attendance states cannot say *"this student was not expected today"*. A student
in a group that trains twice a week who comes once is marked `absent_unexcused` every week
forever, and reads as ~50% attendance while attending everything they signed up for. §5.14's
gap count becomes noise and a manager chases parents who are missing nothing.

Money is already correct — §5.7: *"the monthly fee buys the slot, not the sessions."*
Reporting is not.

The club confirmed **the manager sets, per student, which day or days they come.** So
`enrollment` has to carry that pattern.

### The two are one decision

*"Twice a week"* is simultaneously what a student **attends** (C12) and what they **pay for**
(C11). Settle them together, or the second will contradict the first. Design the pattern
once, on `enrollment`, and let both the billing run and the roster read it.

Neither is designed here on purpose — the contract session owns that, and it has the club's
answer as its input rather than a guess.
