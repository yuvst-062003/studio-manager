THE FIVE RESTRICTIONS, each with a test. These are what make the whole thing safe
to ship, and several are the reason §19 exists at all:
  - Cannot act inside a non-demo studio in production. The studio resolver
    excludes is_demo = false for developer sessions in production.
  - Cannot reach /dev/* in production. Conditionally mounted, not guarded.
  - Cannot read any real person's health declaration. Break-glass excludes health
    contents entirely and the developer flag does not change that.
  - Cannot grant itself the flag, or grant it to anyone. is_developer is settable
    ONLY by seed or migration — assert no route can write that column.
  - Cannot touch live money. The demo studio's uPay config is pinned to
    livesystem=0, and a test asserts a demo studio can never render a live
    payment form.
